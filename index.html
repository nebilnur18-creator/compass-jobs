About Compass

Find Your Direction

Compass Jobs is an Ethiopian job discovery platform created to bring vacancy announcements into one easier-to-search place.

Instead of checking many channels and websites separately, job seekers can explore organized opportunities through Compass Jobs and the Compass Telegram channel.

  • Fresh vacancy discovery
  • Clear job information
  • Searchable categories and locations
  • Direct access to application sources
  • Free access for job seekers
Important note

What AI does — and does not do

Groq AI helps Compass organize and format vacancy information. AI formatting does not automatically mean that an employer or vacancy has been officially verified.

Before applying, check the original source, deadline, employer identity, requirements, and application method.

If you find a suspicious listing, expired link, incorrect information, or possible scam, contact the Compass team through Telegram.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is Compass Jobs free?

Yes. Browsing ordinary job vacancy information is free for job seekers.

How often are jobs updated?

The website checks the job data regularly. New vacancies appear when they are processed and published to the data source.

How do I apply for a job?

Open the vacancy and use the available application or original source link. Follow the employer’s instructions.

Does AI verify every employer?

No. AI helps organize vacancy information. Applicants should still confirm the employer and application details.

Can I report a fake or expired vacancy?

Yes. Contact Compass through the Telegram channel or bot and provide the vacancy link and the problem you found.

Do I need to create an account?

Not for browsing the current job feed. Future features may include saved jobs, alerts, and user accounts.
